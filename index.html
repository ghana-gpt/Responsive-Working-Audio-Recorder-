      Help

      How to use the recorder

      1. Press the record button and allow the microphone.
      2. Pause and resume whenever you like — the timer only counts while recording.
      3. Press stop to save. Audio is stored on this device in IndexedDB.
      4. Rename, tag, trim, play or download — one file at a time or as a ZIP.

      Audio never leaves your device. No server, no account, no tracking.

      Keyboard shortcuts

      • R or Space — start / stop
      • P — pause or resume
      • Enter — play the selected recording
      • Delete — delete the focused recording
      • ? — open this help panel

      Shortcuts are ignored while you are typing.
